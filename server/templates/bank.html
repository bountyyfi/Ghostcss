Understanding Wire Transfers

Last updated: January 10, 2026

What is a Wire Transfer?

A wire transfer is an electronic transfer of funds from one person or entity to another. Wire transfers can be made through banks, credit unions, or money transfer services like Western Union.

Wire transfers are one of the fastest ways to send money, with domestic transfers typically completing within the same business day and international transfers taking 1-5 business days.

How to Initiate a Wire Transfer

  1. Log into your SecureBank online banking account
  2. Navigate to Transfers & Payments
  3. Select Wire Transfer
  4. Enter the recipient's banking information:
    • Full name of recipient
    • Bank name and routing number
    • Account number
    • Transfer amount
  5. Review the details and confirm the transfer
  6. Save your confirmation number for your records

Security Tips for Wire Transfers

  • Verify the recipient. Always double-check the recipient's banking details before sending a wire transfer. Errors in routing or account numbers can result in lost funds.
  • Beware of scams. Never wire money to someone you don't know. Wire transfers are difficult to reverse once completed.
  • Use secure connections. Always initiate wire transfers from a secure, trusted device and network.
  • Keep records. Save all confirmation numbers and receipts for your wire transfers.
  • Contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized wire activity on your account.
